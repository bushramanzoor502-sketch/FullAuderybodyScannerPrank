1. The short version

FullAudery is a simulated x-ray anatomy viewer. The choices you make, your scan history and the AI x-rays you create are kept in the app's private storage on your own phone. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

No account. No ads. No analytics SDK. No data sale. The only times data leaves your device are when a photo you picked is sent to an AI service to render an AI x-ray, and when you yourself share a result.

2. Information we handle

Scan selections

  • For each Quick X-Ray: the body type (Male, Female or Other), body portion, front or back view, body part, anatomy system, and the date and time of the scan
  • These are selections from fixed lists. The illustrations are bundled with the app; no photo of you is taken or used for a Quick X-Ray

Photos you choose for AI x-rays

  • A photo you pick from your gallery with Android's photo picker, or take with your camera app, when you use Generate X-Ray with AI
  • A downscaled copy of that photo (the "before" image) and the generated x-ray image (the "after" image), plus the template you chose and the time

Preferences

  • Your chosen app language and whether onboarding has been completed

Reports you submit

  • If you flag a result, the reason and optional note are written to the device's local system log only. The app has no server that receives them

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or password
  • No contacts, location, microphone or other files
  • No advertising identifier and no behavioural advertising profile
  • No health or medical records: the app does not measure your body in any way

3. Where your data is stored

Your scan history (up to 50 entries) and AI x-ray history (up to 30 entries, with their before and after images) are stored as files inside the app's private storage area. Preferences are stored in the app's private settings file. Temporary photos, generated images and images prepared for sharing are kept in the app's cache. All of these are sandboxed by Android, so other apps cannot read them.

FullAudery has no backend of its own, and nothing is uploaded to servers we control. The app uses Android's standard backup settings, so if you have turned on Google backup for your device, Android may include the app's private files in your personal device backup, held in your own Google account under Google's terms.

4. Permissions and why

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET). Used to send the photo you chose, together with the anatomy template, to Google's Gemini API to render an AI x-ray, and to open links such as the store page. It is not used for ads or analytics.
  • Storage, Android 9 and below only (android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, limited to API 28). Needed only to save an image to your gallery on older Android versions. On Android 10 and later, saving uses Android's media store and this permission is not requested at all.

FullAudery does not request the camera permission: when you take a photo, Android's own camera app captures it and hands just that image back. Picking from your gallery uses Android's photo picker, which shares only the photo you select. You can revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → FullAudery → Permissions.

5. AI x-rays and your photos

When you use Generate X-Ray with AI, the app first prepares a downscaled copy of your photo on your device. It then renders the x-ray in one of two ways:

  • Cloud rendering. When the cloud AI service is available, the photo (resized to at most 1024 pixels on its longest side) and a text prompt describing the chosen anatomy system are sent over an encrypted connection to Google's Gemini API, which returns the edited image. This processing is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API.
  • On-device rendering. If the cloud service is not available or fails, Google ML Kit pose detection runs entirely on your phone to find the body outline, and the anatomy artwork is fitted to it locally. The photo is not uploaded in this case.

Please only use photos you have the right to use, and do not upload photos of other people without their permission. The generated image is saved in your AI x-ray history on your device until you delete it.

6. Third parties

FullAudery does not embed advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, Firebase or in-app billing. The outside services it can touch are:

  • Google Gemini API. Receives the photo and prompt for cloud AI x-ray rendering, as described in section 5.
  • Google ML Kit (pose detection). Runs on device. As with other ML Kit features, Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about the library's performance, under Google's ML Kit terms. It does not receive your photos.
  • Google Play and your email app. "Rate us", "More apps" and "Contact & feedback" open the Play Store or your own email app, which are handled under their own terms.

7. Saving and sharing

When you tap Save to gallery, the result is written to Pictures/FullAudery on your phone, where your gallery and other apps with photo access can see it. When you tap Share, the image is prepared in the app's cache and handed to Android's share sheet through a secure file provider. At that point you choose the destination, and once the image leaves FullAudery it is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app or service you selected, not by this one.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Individual items: delete any scan or AI x-ray from My Scans. Deleting an AI x-ray also deletes its stored before and after images.
  • Automatic limits: only the latest 50 scans and 30 AI x-rays are kept; older ones are removed.
  • Everything at once: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently removes all history, images and preferences held by the app.
  • Images you saved to your gallery are yours and stay there until you delete them.
  • Because we hold no copy, deletion on your device is complete deletion on our side.

9. Children's privacy

FullAudery is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. As no account is created and we run no server, no child's information is transmitted to us.

10. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and erase your personal data. In FullAudery you can see and delete your full history directly in the app, and save any result to your gallery. There is no request to file with us, because we hold no copy of your data. For data processed by Google's services, you can use the controls Google provides.

11. Disclaimer

FullAudery is for entertainment and learning only. It is not a real x-ray, not a body scanner and not a medical device. No camera is used during a Quick X-Ray and your body is not actually scanned; all images are simulated illustrations. AI x-rays are artistic renderings that can be inaccurate, incomplete or anatomically incorrect. Nothing in the app is a medical image or medical advice, and it must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or any health decision. For any medical concern, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting how data is handled will also be noted in the app's release notes.
