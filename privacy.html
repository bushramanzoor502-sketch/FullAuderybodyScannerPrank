1. The short version

Full Audery Body Scanner Prank is a simulated x-ray anatomy viewer. The choices you make, your scan history and the AI x-rays you create are kept in the app's private storage on your own phone. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not include any advertising SDK, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone. The app does include Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, which send back anonymous usage events and crash reports to help us find bugs — never your photos, and never anything that identifies you.

No account. No ads. No data sale. The only times data leaves your device are when a photo you picked is sent to an AI service to render an AI x-ray, when the app sends an anonymous usage event or crash report, and when you yourself share a result.

2. Information we handle

Scan selections

  • For each Quick X-Ray: the body type (Male, Female or Other), body portion, front or back view, body part, anatomy system, and the date and time of the scan
  • These are selections from fixed lists. The illustrations are bundled with the app; no photo of you is taken or used for a Quick X-Ray

Photos you choose for AI x-rays

  • A photo you pick from your gallery with Android's photo picker, or take with the in-app camera, when you use Generate X-Ray with AI
  • A downscaled copy of that photo (the "before" image) and the generated x-ray image (the "after" image), plus the template you chose and the time

Preferences

  • Your chosen app language and whether onboarding has been completed

Reports you submit

  • If you flag a result, the reason and optional note are written to the device's local system log only. The app has no server that receives them

Usage analytics and crash reports

  • The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to send back anonymous events: which screen you opened, that a scan or AI x-ray was started/completed, which anatomy system or photo source you picked, that a result was saved, shared or reported (and the reason), a language change, a settings toggle, or a rate/support link tap
  • If the app crashes, or catches an internal error, a crash report is sent with the screen name where it happened and the technical error details — never your photos, scan content or anything you typed
  • These events use Google's own install-scoped Firebase identifier, not your Google account, an advertising ID or any other identifier tied to you personally, and collection is switched off entirely in the app's internal test builds

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or password
  • No contacts, location, microphone or other files
  • No advertising identifier and no behavioural advertising profile
  • No health or medical records: the app does not measure your body in any way

3. Where your data is stored

Your scan history (up to 50 entries) and AI x-ray history (up to 30 entries, with their before and after images) are stored as files inside the app's private storage area. Preferences are stored in the app's private settings file. Temporary photos, generated images and images prepared for sharing are kept in the app's cache. All of these are sandboxed by Android, so other apps cannot read them.

Full Audery Body Scanner Prank has no backend of its own, and nothing is uploaded to servers we control. The app opts out of Android's automatic backup entirely, so none of its private files — your scan history, AI x-rays or settings — are ever included in a phone backup or your Google Account backup.

4. Permissions and why

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET). Used to send the photo you chose, together with the anatomy template, to Google's Gemini API to render an AI x-ray, to send anonymous Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics events (section 2), and to open links such as the store page. It is never used to show ads.
  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA). Only requested the first time you choose "Camera" as your photo source for an AI x-ray, or during onboarding. It lets you take a photo directly in the app instead of switching to a separate camera app; nothing is captured without you starting it, and the app cannot access the camera at any other time.
  • Storage, Android 9 and below only (android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, limited to API 28). Needed only to save an image to your gallery on older Android versions. On Android 10 and later, saving uses Android's media store and this permission is not requested at all.

Picking from your gallery instead uses Android's photo picker, which shares only the photo you select — the app never sees the rest of your gallery. You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Full Audery Body Scanner Prank → Permissions.

5. AI x-rays and your photos

When you use Generate X-Ray with AI, the app first prepares a downscaled copy of your photo on your device. It then renders the x-ray in one of two ways:

  • Cloud rendering. When the cloud AI service is available, the photo (resized to at most 1024 pixels on its longest side) and a text prompt describing the chosen anatomy system are sent over an encrypted connection to Google's Gemini API, which returns the edited image. This processing is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API.
  • On-device rendering. If the cloud service is not available or fails, Google ML Kit pose detection runs entirely on your phone to find the body outline, and the anatomy artwork is fitted to it locally. The photo is not uploaded in this case.

Please only use photos you have the right to use, and do not upload photos of other people without their permission. The generated image is saved in your AI x-ray history on your device until you delete it.

What the effect actually does. Despite the playful "prank" branding, the app does not remove, alter or reveal anything under a person's clothing, and it cannot generate nudity. Every anatomy prompt explicitly instructs the model to keep the same person, pose, clothing and background untouched, and to overlay a semi-transparent, glowing x-ray/skeleton illustration over the photo, as a visual effect only — the clothing stays fully visible in the result. It is a novelty photo filter, not a real scanning or undressing tool.

6. Third parties

Full Audery Body Scanner Prank does not embed any advertising network or in-app billing. The outside services it can touch are:

  • Google Gemini API. Receives the photo and prompt for cloud AI x-ray rendering, as described in section 5.
  • Google ML Kit (pose detection). Runs on device. As with other ML Kit features, Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about the library's performance, under Google's ML Kit terms. It does not receive your photos.
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Receive the anonymous usage events and crash reports described in section 2, under Google's Firebase terms and privacy policy. The app strips the advertising ID permission and explicitly disables ad-ID collection and ad personalization signals in its Analytics configuration, so these events are never linked to advertising or used to build an ad profile.
  • Google Play and your email app. "Rate us", "More apps" and "Contact & feedback" open the Play Store or your own email app, which are handled under their own terms.

7. Saving and sharing

When you tap Save to gallery, the result is written to Pictures/Full Audery on your phone, where your gallery and other apps with photo access can see it. When you tap Share, the image is prepared in the app's cache and handed to Android's share sheet through a secure file provider. At that point you choose the destination, and once the image leaves Full Audery it is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app or service you selected, not by this one.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Individual items: delete any scan or AI x-ray from My Scans. Deleting an AI x-ray also deletes its stored before and after images.
  • Automatic limits: only the latest 50 scans and 30 AI x-rays are kept; older ones are removed.
  • Everything at once: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently removes all history, images and preferences held by the app.
  • Images you saved to your gallery are yours and stay there until you delete them.
  • Because we hold no copy, deletion on your device is complete deletion on our side.
  • Anonymous Firebase Analytics events and Crashlytics reports are not tied to your identity, so there is no per-person record to delete; Google retains this aggregate data under its own Firebase data-retention settings, separately from anything on your device.

9. Children's privacy

Full Audery Body Scanner Prank is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. No account is created and we run no server of our own; the only data that leaves the device is described in sections 2 and 6, none of which identifies who sent it.

10. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and erase your personal data. In Full Audery Body Scanner Prank you can see and delete your full history directly in the app, and save any result to your gallery. We hold no personal data of yours to act on, and the anonymous analytics events described in section 2 are not linked to your identity, so there is nothing to individually access or erase on request. For data processed by Google's services (Gemini, ML Kit, Firebase), you can use the controls Google provides.

11. Disclaimer

Full Audery Body Scanner Prank is for entertainment and pranks only. It is not a real x-ray, not a body scanner, not a clothing-removal tool and not a medical device. No camera is used during a Quick X-Ray and your body is not actually scanned; all images are simulated illustrations. AI x-rays are artistic renderings that can be inaccurate, incomplete or anatomically incorrect, and clothing always stays visible in the result — nothing is undressed or revealed. Nothing in the app is a medical image or medical advice, and it must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or any health decision. For any medical concern, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
